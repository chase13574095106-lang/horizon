## [Microsoft Launches MAI-Thinking-1 and MAI-Code-1-Flash LLMs](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/2/microsofts-new-models/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Microsoft announced two new text LLMs: MAI-Thinking-1, a 35B-parameter reasoning model, and MAI-Code-1-Flash, a 5B-parameter code model, both trained on clean, commercially licensed data without distillation from third-party models. These models achieve competitive performance with much larger models at a fraction of the parameter count, potentially lowering inference costs and enabling local deployment. MAI-Code-1-Flash is already rolling out to GitHub Copilot users in VS Code, signaling a shift toward efficient, specialized models. MAI-Thinking-1 is a sparse Mixture of Experts model with 35B active parameters and ~1T total parameters, and it is preferred to Sonnet 4.6 in blind human evaluations. MAI-Code-1-Flash uses adaptive solution length control to adjust response depth based on task complexity.

rss · Simon Willison · Jun 2, 22:21

**Background**: Large language models (LLMs) are typically measured by their parameter count, which correlates with capability but also cost and compute requirements. Most code models are trained on unlicensed web data, raising copyright concerns. Microsoft's claim of using 'appropriately licensed' data is a notable departure.

## [Tiger Brokers Halts New Trades for Mainland China Accounts](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/LgwHvOhuFw338kvWPgPyvw) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tiger Brokers announced that starting June 12, 2026, it will suspend new positions and additional trades for all securities types in mainland China accounts, allowing only sell and close operations, and will also halt inbound fund transfers while outbound transfers remain unaffected. This move signals intensified regulatory enforcement on cross-border securities trading, directly impacting Chinese investors who use Tiger Brokers to trade overseas stocks, and reflects the ongoing crackdown on unlicensed cross-border brokerage services. The adjustment applies only to accounts serviced within mainland China; existing overseas services and asset safety remain unaffected. The suspension follows a two-year centralized rectification period mandated by regulators.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 2, 12:56

**Background**: Tiger Brokers and similar platforms like Futu have faced regulatory scrutiny from Chinese authorities for facilitating cross-border securities trading without proper licenses. In 2023, they were fined over 2.2 billion yuan collectively. The new measures are part of a broader effort to enforce compliance and protect domestic investors.
