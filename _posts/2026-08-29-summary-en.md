## [DHS Uses Obscure Law to Secretly Obtain Journalists' Records](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/29/trump-dhs-1509-summons-records-journalists-nonprofits) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Trump administration's Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has been using an obscure legal provision, known as a 1509 summons, to secretly obtain private records of journalists, non-profits, and unions without judicial oversight. In one case, DHS obtained six months of phone records for Minneapolis journalist Georgia Fort from T-Mobile, including over 10,000 calls and texts. This raises serious concerns about press freedom and privacy, as the government can access sensitive communications without a warrant or prior notification. It could have a chilling effect on journalism and activism, and may set a precedent for broader surveillance of dissenting voices. The 1509 summons is typically used to inspect records for customs and duty enforcement, but DHS has repurposed it for surveillance. In several cases, DHS withdrew the summons after legal challenges, possibly to avoid a judicial ruling on its legality. Notably, T-Mobile complied with the request, while Google did not.

hackernews · firefax · Aug 29, 18:44 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49492219)

**Background**: The 1509 summons is derived from a law that grants DHS broad authority to inspect records to ensure proper collection of duties and taxes on imports. This authority has been used in a surveillance context, allowing the agency to obtain records from telecom and social media companies without a court order. The practice has drawn criticism from civil liberties advocates, who argue it circumvents the Fourth Amendment protections against unreasonable searches.

## [OpenAI Ends Cursor Partnership Over SpaceX Acquisition, Sets Shutdown Date](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI announced it is terminating its contract to provide models to Cursor following SpaceX's acquisition, proposing a shutdown date of November 12, 2026. The company cited concerns that SpaceX would not comply with its service terms, referencing past contract violations by Musk-owned companies. This decision disrupts the AI coding tools market, affecting developers who rely on OpenAI models within Cursor. It also highlights growing tensions between OpenAI and Elon Musk's companies, potentially reshaping industry partnerships and trust dynamics. OpenAI's custom agreement with Cursor allowed termination within a limited time after a change of control, and the two had partnered for nearly four years. OpenAI cited Musk's track record, including Twitter's post-acquisition contract violations and xAI's admission of violating OpenAI's service terms earlier this year.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 29, 02:24

**Background**: Cursor is an AI-powered code editor that integrates models from providers like OpenAI and Anthropic. SpaceX, led by Elon Musk, recently acquired Cursor's developer Anysphere for $60 billion in an all-stock deal. OpenAI's decision stems from a lack of trust in Musk's companies to adhere to service agreements, reflecting broader industry concerns about AI neutrality and consolidation.
