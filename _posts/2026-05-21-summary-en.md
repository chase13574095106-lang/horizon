## [OpenAI Adds Google SynthID Watermark to ChatGPT Images](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/933442/openai-synthid-content-credentials-c2pa-expansion) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has started embedding Google's SynthID watermark into images generated by ChatGPT, Codex, and the OpenAI API, in addition to C2PA metadata, and launched a public verification tool to check for these marks. This dual-layer approach (C2PA metadata + SynthID watermark) significantly strengthens content provenance for AI-generated images, making it harder to strip away origin information and helping combat misinformation. SynthID watermarks are designed to survive screenshots and simple transformations, while C2PA metadata can be accidentally stripped by platform compression; the verification tool currently only detects OpenAI's own marks, and a negative result does not guarantee the image is not AI-generated.

telegram · zaihuapd · May 21, 02:00

**Background**: Content provenance refers to the ability to trace the origin and history of digital content. C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) is an open standard for embedding metadata, while SynthID is Google DeepMind's invisible watermarking technology for AI-generated content. Both aim to help users distinguish AI-generated content from human-created material.
