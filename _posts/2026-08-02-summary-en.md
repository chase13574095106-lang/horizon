## [Karpathy Highlights 'Pelican on a Bicycle' as AI Physical World Benchmark](https://twitter.com/karpathy/status/2083749667410727319) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Andrej Karpathy tweeted about the 'pelican on a bicycle' benchmark, which involves generating an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle, as a way to expose AI models' understanding of the physical world. This sparked a debate on AI evaluation methods and output quality. This benchmark highlights a shift from simple image generation to more complex tasks that require physical world understanding, which is crucial for advancing AI capabilities. It provides a qualitative measure for comparing model progress and could influence future benchmarking practices. The benchmark was created by developer Simon Willison in late 2024, using the prompt 'Generate an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle.' It has been used to evaluate various LLMs, with results displayed at different temperatures on platforms like Hugging Face.

hackernews · delichon · Aug 2, 04:05 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49140998)

**Background**: Large language models (LLMs) have advanced to generate code and images, but their understanding of the physical world remains limited. Benchmarks like 'pelican on a bicycle' test models' ability to translate a simple scene description into a coherent visual representation, revealing gaps in spatial reasoning and object interaction. Karpathy, a prominent AI researcher, has long emphasized the need for models to understand the physical world beyond text.
