## [Jon Udell Flips Script: Agents Join Human Loops](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/28/jon-udell/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Jon Udell proposes reframing 'human in the loop' as 'agent in the loop', arguing that humans should invite AI agents into their existing workflows rather than being excluded from automated loops. This reframing shifts the narrative from human oversight of machines to human agency, empowering developers to maintain control and transparency in agentic software development. It challenges the prevailing black-box approach and promotes collaborative human-AI workflows. Udell emphasizes that agent-assisted processes should not be black boxes that take prompts and emit features; instead, they should be transparent and integrated into existing human workflows. He uses the analogy of inviting agents onto a team rather than being excluded from the loop.

rss · Simon Willison · Jun 28, 21:57

**Background**: The term 'human in the loop' (HITL) traditionally describes systems where human oversight is required for AI decisions. However, as AI agents become more autonomous, the phrase can imply humans are secondary. Udell's 'agent in the loop' flips this, positioning humans as the primary actors who invite agents to assist. This aligns with broader discussions on agentic software development, where AI agents actively participate in coding, testing, and deployment.

## [CCTV Exposes Systematic Cheating in Phone Reviews](https://weibo.com/2656274875/5314693197725859) ⭐️ 8.0/10

CCTV's investigation reveals that smartphone manufacturers provide special review units with firmware that identifies reviewers and automatically boosts performance, while cloud servers remotely deliver cheating configurations to fake benchmark results. This systemic fraud undermines consumer trust in tech reviews and distorts purchasing decisions, as retail devices perform significantly worse than reviewed units. It also highlights a regulatory gap in the highly technical review industry. The cheating system operates in three layers: hardware screening for review units, firmware-level reviewer identification, and cloud-based real-time performance tuning. When a reviewer is detected, the device raises CPU frequency, increases screen brightness, and loads only UI shells instead of full apps to simulate smoothness.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 28, 01:37

**Background**: Tech product reviews heavily influence consumer choices, especially for smartphones. Manufacturers have long been suspected of sending optimized review units, but this is the first time a systematic three-layer cheating mechanism has been publicly documented by state media, making it difficult for ordinary users to detect.

## [Google Restricts Meta's Gemini Access Due to Compute Shortage](https://www.ft.com/content/c5d52f72-71ef-40bc-bad3-61afdba8b378) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google has restricted Meta's access to its Gemini AI model since March 2026, citing insufficient compute capacity to fulfill Meta's purchased capacity, delaying some of Meta's internal AI projects. This highlights a severe AI compute bottleneck between two tech giants, forcing Meta to accelerate its own model development and potentially reshaping the AI infrastructure landscape. Google signed a $920 million per month compute lease with SpaceX earlier this month to expand capacity, while Meta has begun prioritizing its new Muse Spark model to reduce reliance on external models.
