## [NVIDIA Halts AI Server Shipments to Four Southeast Asian Countries](https://t.me/zaihuapd/45392) ⭐️ 8.0/10

NVIDIA has instructed major manufacturing partners, including Dell and Supermicro, to pause shipments of AI servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia. The freeze is linked to new US export controls and it remains unclear whether it is a temporary measure. The four countries have served as transshipment hubs for Chinese AI companies to obtain NVIDIA's most advanced processors, which cannot be exported directly to mainland China. The halt could disrupt global AI hardware supply chains and data center buildouts in Southeast Asia, while further restricting China's access to advanced AI computing power. The US Commerce Department's new rule requires regional cloud service providers to obtain export licenses within 90 days and verify that their customers do not include restricted Chinese entities. The pause affects servers assembled by OEMs such as Dell and Supermicro, which package NVIDIA chips into complete systems.

**Background**: The US Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) restricts exports of advanced AI chips to China to prevent their use in military and surveillance applications. To circumvent these controls, Chinese firms have routed purchases through third countries, prompting the US to tighten rules on transshipment and remote access. The new interim final rule targets data centers in Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand, requiring licenses for providing AI compute to China-linked entities.
