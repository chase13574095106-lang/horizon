## [Terence Tao: AI has changed mathematics, community must adapt](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/01/mathematics-has-changed-lets-act-like-it/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

In a November 2026 blog post, Terence Tao argues that AI has fundamentally transformed mathematical practice and that the community must update its norms, credit structures, and social foundations accordingly. He contends that substantial credit should go to those who use AI to obtain and thoroughly understand proofs and develop new directions, even without supplying new proof ingredients. Tao is one of the world's leading mathematicians, and his call signals a paradigm shift in how mathematical work is done, credited, and taught. The post affects researchers, journals, funders, and students who must rethink what constitutes a mathematical contribution in an AI-assisted era. Tao emphasizes that 'digestion' of AI-generated results—understanding them thoroughly and building further questions—is itself a substantial contribution, and he warns that the race for answers may harm mathematics. The discussion also notes that AI proofs are often ugly, long, and incomprehensible, though first proofs of major results have historically been similar before elegance emerges.

**Background**: Beginning in the mid-2020s, large language models and reasoning models have made increasing progress in generating research-level mathematical proofs, often using formal languages like Lean where a compiler verifies each step. This has left many mathematicians reeling and prompted debates about credit, elegance, and the social foundations of the field. Terence Tao has written and spoken extensively on how AI should reshape mathematical practice.
