## [Telegram's t.me domain suspended by .me registry](https://www.whois.com/whois/t.me) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Telegram's t.me domain has been suspended, with WHOIS showing serverHold status, indicating the .me registry took action. The suspension likely stems from legal or regulatory investigations in Russia, France, or India. This suspension could disrupt access to Telegram's short links for millions of users, highlighting the vulnerability of centralized domain infrastructure. It also underscores the growing legal pressures on Telegram from multiple countries. The domain status codes include clientRenewProhibited and serverDeleteProhibited, which are typically used during legal disputes. Telegram uses GoDaddy as its registrar, but the serverHold action was taken by the .me registry, not GoDaddy.

**Background**: Domain suspension occurs when a registry or registrar places a domain on hold, making it inaccessible. The .me registry manages the .me top-level domain. Telegram's t.me domain is used for short links and channel invitations. Legal investigations in Russia, France, and India have targeted Telegram for various alleged violations.

## [Samsung Health Threatens Data Deletion Over AI Training Opt-Out](https://neow.in/cWsyMTV3) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Samsung Health now requires users to consent to AI training on their health data, or face deletion of all synced data and loss of cloud sync functionality. This policy raises serious privacy and user rights concerns, as it forces users to choose between losing their health data or allowing Samsung to use sensitive medical information for AI training. The data categories targeted include sleep, medications, medical records, and cycle tracking details; opting out triggers a pop-up warning that syncing will stop and data will be deleted unless retained pursuant to legal obligations.

**Background**: Samsung Health is a popular fitness and health tracking app used with Galaxy Watches and smartphones. AI training on user data helps improve health algorithms and features, but the opt-out penalty has drawn criticism for being coercive and user-hostile.
