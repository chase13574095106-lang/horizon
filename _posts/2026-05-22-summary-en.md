## [Cleve Moler, MATLAB Creator and MathWorks Co-Founder, Dies](https://www.mathworks.com/company/aboutus/founders/clevemoler.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Cleve Moler, co-founder of MathWorks and creator of the MATLAB programming language, has passed away, as announced by MathWorks on their website. Moler's creation of MATLAB revolutionized numerical computing and linear algebra, making advanced mathematics accessible to engineers and scientists worldwide. His passing marks the end of an era for the computational science community. Moler originally wrote MATLAB in the late 1970s as a teaching tool, consisting of about 2,000 lines of FORTRAN code. He co-founded MathWorks in 1984 with Jack Little, and MATLAB has since become a cornerstone of technical computing.

**Background**: MATLAB (short for MATrix LABoratory) is a high-level programming language and interactive environment for numerical computation, visualization, and programming. It was designed to provide easy access to matrix software developed in the LINPACK and EISPACK projects, which Moler helped create. The language has become a standard tool in academia and industry for data analysis, algorithm development, and simulation.

## [Eli Lilly's Retatrutide Phase 3 Trial Shows 28.3% Weight Loss](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lillys-triple-agonist-retatrutide-delivered-powerful-weight-loss-in-pivotal-phase-3-obesity-trial-302778859.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Eli Lilly announced results from the TRIUMPH-1 Phase 3 trial of retatrutide, a triple agonist, showing up to 28.3% average weight loss at the 12 mg dose over 80 weeks in adults with obesity or overweight and at least one weight-related comorbidity without diabetes. This result represents one of the highest average weight losses ever reported in a Phase 3 obesity trial, potentially setting a new benchmark for pharmacologic weight loss and offering a powerful new option for patients with obesity. In the 12 mg group, 45.3% of participants lost at least 30% of their body weight, while the 4 mg group achieved 19.0% average weight loss. The discontinuation rate due to adverse events was 4.1% for retatrutide, lower than the 4.9% in the placebo group.

**Background**: Retatrutide is a triple agonist targeting GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors, which enhances insulin secretion, reduces appetite, and increases energy expenditure. The TRIUMPH-1 trial enrolled approximately 2,500 participants and is part of Eli Lilly's Phase 3 development program for retatrutide in obesity.

## [AI Memory Demand Drives Consumer Electronics Price Hikes](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/22/memory-shortage/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Memory manufacturers are reallocating wafer capacity from DDR and LPDDR to HBM due to surging AI demand, causing a shortage of consumer-device RAM and price increases, especially in sub-$100 smartphones. This structural shift means consumer electronics like smartphones, laptops, and desktops will become significantly more expensive for years, impacting affordability in emerging markets and potentially slowing adoption of new devices. HBM consumes over three times the wafer capacity per gigabyte compared to DDR or LPDDR, and its allocation is expected to rise from 2% to 20% of total wafer capacity by end of 2026. Memory companies deliberately under-provision capacity to avoid overinvestment, prolonging the shortage.

rss · Simon Willison · May 22, 22:01

**Background**: DRAM memory comes in three main types: DDR (used in PCs/servers), LPDDR (used in mobile devices), and HBM (high-bandwidth memory used in AI accelerators). Memory manufacturing is dominated by just three companies (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron), which have limited wafer fabrication capacity. The AI boom has dramatically increased demand for HBM, forcing manufacturers to shift capacity away from consumer memory types.

## [ByteDance Open-Sources Lance, a 3B Unified Multimodal Model](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Xbfq72cr1796RZxJIs3L1A) ⭐️ 8.0/10

ByteDance has open-sourced Lance, a 3B-parameter unified multimodal model that handles image/video understanding, generation, and editing in a single framework. The model is released under the Apache 2.0 license with weights available on Hugging Face. Lance demonstrates that a relatively small 3B model can achieve competitive results on benchmarks like GenEval and VBench, challenging the notion that large models are necessary for multimodal tasks. Its open-source release under Apache 2.0 lowers the barrier for researchers and developers to build upon unified multimodal AI. Lance uses a shared context and dual-stream expert architecture, with Qwen2.5-VL for understanding and Wan2.2 for generation, along with modality-aware positional encoding to resolve sequence boundary confusion. The model has only 3B active parameters but supports five tasks: image understanding, video understanding, image generation, video generation, and cross-modal editing.

telegram · zaihuapd · May 22, 06:40

**Background**: Traditional multimodal models often specialize in either understanding (e.g., image captioning) or generation (e.g., text-to-image), requiring separate models for different tasks. Unified models aim to combine both capabilities in a single architecture, but they typically require large parameter counts. Lance achieves unification at a 3B scale by leveraging existing strong encoders (Qwen2.5-VL and Wan2.2) and a novel dual-stream design.
