Developer Patrick McCanna claims that Claude Code's 'Extended Thinking' output is a lossy summary of the model's reasoning, not an authentic audit trail, raising concerns about transparency and security. This matters because hidden reasoning can obscure prompt injection attacks and make it harder for users to trust and optimize AI models, affecting safety and competitive fairness in the AI industry. The summary is compared to converting a lossless BMP to a lossy JPEG, causing data loss. Hidden reasoning also allows models to call functions during the hidden phase, potentially exfiltrating data.

**Background**: Extended thinking is a feature in Claude models that allows the model to reason step-by-step before responding. However, the output shown to users is a compressed summary, not the full reasoning chain. This practice is common among major AI companies like OpenAI and Google to protect proprietary reasoning techniques.

## [Deno Desktop: Build Desktop Apps with Deno](https://docs.deno.com/runtime/desktop/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Deno has introduced a new `deno desktop` command that compiles a Deno project into a self-contained desktop application, bundling code, the Deno runtime, and a web rendering engine into a single binary per platform. This enables developers to build cross-platform desktop apps using web technologies with Deno's security model, offering multiple backends (CEF, Webview, Raw) and a planned shared CEF runtime to reduce binary sizes, potentially challenging Electron and Tauri. The `deno desktop` command supports framework auto-detection (Next.js, Astro, SvelteKit, etc.) and uses in-process bindings instead of socket-based IPC. A shared CEF runtime is on the roadmap to drop binary sizes to a few MB per app.

hackernews · GeneralMaximus · Jun 22, 05:38 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48626137)

**Background**: Deno is a modern runtime for JavaScript and TypeScript built on V8 and Rust, known for its security-first approach with explicit permission system. Desktop app development with web technologies traditionally relies on Electron (bundles Chromium) or Tauri (uses system webview), each with trade-offs in binary size and performance.

## [48 Chinese Developers File Antitrust Complaint Against Apple](https://m.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-06-22/4433380.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On June 22, 2026, 48 Chinese iOS developers filed an antitrust complaint with China's State Administration for Market Regulation, accusing Apple of abusing its monopoly position in China and failing to honor its commitment that App Store fees in China would not exceed the global average. This complaint could trigger regulatory action in China, Apple's second-largest iOS market, potentially forcing the company to open third-party distribution and payment channels, which would significantly reduce the 'Apple tax' burden on developers and reshape the app economy. Although Apple lowered its standard commission rate in China to 25% in March 2026, developers still lack access to third-party app stores, alternative in-app payment systems, and external link payments, limiting real bargaining power.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 22, 14:57

**Background**: Apple's App Store charges developers a commission on digital purchases, commonly referred to as the 'Apple tax.' In China, the standard rate was historically 30%, but was reduced to 25% in March 2026. Globally, Apple faces increasing antitrust scrutiny over its closed ecosystem, with regulators in the EU, US, and other markets pushing for more openness. The complaint highlights the disparity between China and markets like Brazil, where third-party distribution and payment options are already available.
