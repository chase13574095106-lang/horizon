## [Isar Aerospace Reaches Orbit, Deploys Payloads on Second Flight](https://isaraerospace.com/press/history-for-european-spaceflight-isar-aerospace-reaches-orbit-and-deploys-payloads-on-second-flight) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Isar Aerospace successfully launched its Spectrum rocket into low Earth orbit from the Andøya Spaceport in Norway on its second flight, deploying five small satellites and an experimental payload. This marks the first time a privately developed rocket has reached orbit from mainland Europe. This milestone provides Europe with sovereign commercial access to space, reducing reliance on foreign launch providers and Arianespace. It signals a new era for European spaceflight and positions Isar Aerospace as a competitive player in the global small-satellite launch market. The Spectrum rocket is a two-stage, 28-meter-tall vehicle designed for small satellites, with a payload capacity of around 1,000 kg to low Earth orbit. The successful flight follows a first attempt in March 2025 that ended in failure shortly after liftoff.

**Background**: Isar Aerospace was founded by three students from the Technical University of Munich and is headquartered in Ottobrunn, Germany. The company is part of a wave of European commercial launch startups aiming to provide independent access to space, a capability historically dominated by government-backed entities like Arianespace.

## [Asahi Linux Announces Official Support for Apple M3 Chips](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Asahi Linux has announced official support for Apple's M3 series chips (M3, M3 Pro, M3 Max), marking a major milestone in running Linux on Apple Silicon. This support was enabled through nearly 1,000 patches merged into the Linux 7.2 kernel, making the M3 SoCs work with the mainline kernel. This development is significant for the Linux-on-ARM community as it expands the range of Apple Silicon hardware that can run Linux with official support, potentially increasing adoption among developers and enthusiasts. It demonstrates substantial technical progress in reverse-engineering Apple's proprietary hardware, which could pave the way for future support of newer chips. The support is currently available in the linux-asahi kernel and is being upstreamed to the mainline kernel, with feature support details documented on the Asahi Linux wiki. However, some features like GPU acceleration, sleep, and HDMI output may still be incomplete or in progress, as noted in community discussions.

hackernews · mdp2021 · Sep 6, 14:08 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)

**Background**: Asahi Linux is a project that ports the Linux kernel and related software to Apple Silicon-powered Macs, started by Hector Martin. It relies on reverse-engineering Apple's SoCs, which lack official public documentation. The project has been working for years to bring Linux to Apple's M1, M2, and now M3 chips, with each generation presenting new challenges.

## [China Approves First AI-Assisted Innovative Drug for COVID-19](https://www.gelonghui.com/live/2653282) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China's National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) granted conditional approval to the first AI-assisted innovative drug, Yisitevir tablets (brand name: Aipusiwei), developed by Westlake University, Westlake Laboratory, and Westlake Pharmaceutical (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. The drug is intended for the treatment of mild to moderate COVID-19 in adults, and it took only 3.5 years from initial discovery to completion of clinical trials. This milestone demonstrates the practical application of AI in pharmaceutical R&D, potentially accelerating drug discovery timelines and reducing costs. It could encourage broader adoption of AI-assisted methods in China's pharmaceutical industry and influence global trends in AI-driven drug development. The drug received conditional approval, which is typically granted based on early clinical data and requires further confirmatory trials. The development journey from discovery to clinical trials was completed in just 3.5 years, significantly shorter than the traditional 10-15 years for new drugs.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 6, 09:10

**Background**: Traditional drug discovery is a lengthy, costly, and high-risk process, often taking over a decade and billions of dollars. AI and machine learning can accelerate this by predicting drug-target interactions, optimizing lead compounds, and assessing safety and toxicity early in the pipeline. Conditional approval by the NMPA is a regulatory pathway that allows early access to promising drugs for serious conditions while requiring post-marketing studies.
