## [F-Droid Labels Google ADV as Malware on 4B Devices](https://f-droid.org/2026/07/01/adv-malware.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

F-Droid has officially classified Google's Android Developer Verification (ADV) as malware, warning that it will block unapproved apps on approximately 4 billion Android devices starting in late 2026. This marks a significant escalation in the conflict between Google and the open-source community, potentially restricting software freedom and sideloading on billions of devices, affecting developers and users who rely on alternative app stores like F-Droid. ADV is a system process with root privileges that cannot be removed, and it will first activate in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand on September 30, 2026, with global rollout planned for 2027 and beyond.

This shift makes Hong Kong a critical hub for AI chip trade into China, highlighting its geopolitical risk amid US-China tensions and reshaping global semiconductor supply chains. AI-related electronics now account for 57% to 70% of Hong Kong's exports, and the Hong Kong Trade Development Council raised its 2026 export growth forecast to over 20%.

## [Fudan Course Replaces Exam with Human vs. AI Test](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/d53O-6mVFZqMa_Sti1yEPw) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Fudan University's 'Data Mining Techniques' course replaced its final exam with a 'human vs. AI' test, where 51 students each created 10 computational questions to stump three AI models; the more the AI got wrong, the higher the student's score. Only 4 students managed to make a model score zero, and the strongest model, Claude, was never fully defeated. This innovative assessment reflects a paradigm shift in education for the AI era, moving from testing rote memorization to evaluating students' ability to design problems, judge AI outputs, and think critically. It signals that future education will focus on teaching students how to command and evaluate AI rather than just using algorithms. The class average score was 85.7 out of 100, and 50 out of 51 students managed to stump at least one AI model on at least one question. The three AI models tested were not named in the report, but Claude (by Anthropic) was identified as the strongest, as no student's set of questions made it score zero.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 5, 08:40

**Background**: Traditional exams in data mining courses often test algorithmic knowledge and memorization, which become less relevant as AI tools can perform many routine tasks. The instructor, Xiao Yanghua, emphasized that in the AI era, assessment should focus on evaluation, judgment, and creative thinking. This approach aligns with a broader trend in education to integrate AI literacy and critical thinking into curricula.
