## [China's First Mandatory L3/L4 Autonomous Driving Standard Submitted for Approval](https://www.sohu.com/a/1038536454_115362) ⭐️ 9.0/10

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology has completed the draft of the mandatory national standard 'Safety Requirements for Intelligent Connected Vehicle Autonomous Driving Systems' and submitted it for approval, with a proposed implementation date of July 1, 2027. This is China's first mandatory standard for L3 and L4 autonomous driving, introducing a Safety Case mechanism that requires companies to systematically demonstrate safety using a 'claim-argument-evidence' structure. This standard marks a shift from vague regulatory concepts to hard safety constraints, ending the era of ambiguous marketing claims by automakers and focusing competition on safety capabilities. It will likely increase short-term costs for redundant systems and high-performance chips, but these costs are expected to be amortized through technological iteration and economies of scale. The standard specifically addresses human-machine handover for L3 systems and autonomous risk handling for L4 systems. It is currently in the public comment period starting June 17, with the proposed effective date of July 1, 2027.

**Background**: Autonomous driving levels are defined by SAE International: L3 (conditional automation) allows the vehicle to drive itself under certain conditions but requires the driver to take over when requested; L4 (high automation) can handle all driving tasks within a defined operational design domain without driver intervention. A Safety Case is a structured argument supported by evidence to demonstrate that a system is acceptably safe for a specific application in a specific operating environment.

## [3D Fiber Micro-Tweezer Achieves 100,000x Force Boost](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-06/19/content_534836.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers from Anhui University and the University of Science and Technology of China developed a 3D fiber micro-tweezer using femtosecond laser fabrication, achieving output forces 100,000 times greater than traditional optical tweezers. The device integrates light transmission, photothermal conversion, material response, and mechanical output on a single commercial optical fiber. This breakthrough overcomes the fundamental limitation of weak forces in traditional optical tweezers, enabling high-precision 3D manipulation of single cells and opaque objects. It opens new possibilities for biomedical research, microsurgery, and minimally invasive medical procedures. The micro-tweezer's force is continuously controllable by adjusting input light power, and it can perform precise sampling in spaces as small as 100 micrometers. The work was published in the journal Nature.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 20, 15:19

**Background**: Traditional optical tweezers use a tightly focused laser beam to trap and manipulate microscopic particles, but they suffer from weak forces (typically piconewtons) and cannot handle opaque objects. Fiber-based optical tweezers offer compactness and flexibility, but previous designs had limited force output. Femtosecond laser fabrication enables precise 3D microstructuring on fiber tips, allowing the creation of complex mechanical structures that convert light into strong mechanical forces.

## [IETF Proposes HTTP QUERY Method for Safe Queries with Body](https://httpwg.org/http-extensions/draft-ietf-httpbis-safe-method-w-body.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The IETF HTTP Working Group is developing a draft to add a new HTTP QUERY method, which allows sending query parameters in the request body while maintaining the safety and idempotency of GET, enabling caching, retries, and automatic recovery. This proposal addresses real-world limitations of GET (URL length constraints) and POST (non-idempotent, not cacheable), potentially simplifying API design and improving performance for complex queries in web applications. The QUERY method defines an Accept-Query response header for servers to declare supported query formats, and the current draft expires in December 2026.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 20, 06:28

**Background**: HTTP GET requests are limited by URL length and cannot carry a body, while POST requests are not safe or idempotent, making them unsuitable for caching and retries. The proposed QUERY method combines the benefits of both, allowing complex queries in the body with safe semantics.
