## [Apple Withholds Siri from EU After Exemption Denied](https://www.reuters.com/business/apple-failed-make-its-ai-tool-comply-eu-regulations-eu-commission-says-2026-06-09/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple decided not to launch its enhanced Siri features in the European Union after EU regulators denied its request for an 18-month exemption from compliance with the Digital Markets Act. This decision highlights the growing tension between big tech companies and EU regulators over data privacy and market fairness, potentially limiting EU consumers' access to advanced AI features and setting a precedent for future product launches. Apple had requested an exemption to avoid opening up its ecosystem as required by the Digital Markets Act, but the EU Commission denied the request, leading Apple to withhold Siri rather than comply.

hackernews · flanged · Jun 9, 16:13 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463024)

**Background**: The Digital Markets Act (DMA) is an EU regulation that aims to curb the power of large online platforms by requiring them to be more open and interoperable. Apple's Siri features, which rely on deep integration with the device's operating system, would need to comply with DMA rules that mandate allowing third-party access to certain functionalities. Apple argued that compliance would compromise user privacy and security.

**Discussion**: Community comments are mixed: some users support Apple's stance on privacy, while others criticize Apple for blaming the EU and see the move as a strategic play for consumer sympathy. Some European users view this as an opportunity for local alternatives.

## [Xiaomi Releases 1T-Parameter MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed at 1000 tokens/s](https://platform.xiaomimimo.com/docs/en-US/model-intro/mimo-v2.5-pro-ultraspeed) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Xiaomi has released MiMo-V2.5-Pro-UltraSpeed, a 1-trillion-parameter large language model that achieves an inference speed of 1000 tokens per second through FP4 mixed-precision quantization and DFlash speculative decoding. This breakthrough makes trillion-parameter models viable for latency-sensitive applications like quantitative trading and real-time risk control, potentially expanding the use of large models in high-stakes decision-making scenarios. The API is available for a limited trial from June 9 to 23, with an application-based approval system, limited to 10 queue requests per day and 30 minutes per session, prioritizing enterprise users. The trial price is about three times that of the standard MiMo-V2.5-Pro, while speed is roughly 10 times faster.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 9, 03:26

**Background**: Large language models (LLMs) with trillions of parameters typically require significant computational resources, leading to high latency during inference. FP4 quantization reduces model precision to 4-bit floating-point, shrinking memory footprint and accelerating computation, while speculative decoding uses a smaller draft model to propose tokens that the main model verifies in parallel, further reducing latency. TileRT is a tile-based runtime engine designed for ultra-low-latency LLM inference.

## [China Plans $295B National Computing Network with Domestic AI Chips](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3353891/china-ramps-building-national-computing-power-network-ai-token-demand-surges) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China plans to invest 2 trillion yuan ($295 billion) over five years to build a national computing power network, with state-owned telecom operators managing major facilities. The plan prioritizes domestic AI chips from suppliers like Huawei for at least 80% of the infrastructure, aiming to reduce reliance on U.S. companies such as Nvidia and AMD. This massive investment signals China's strategic push to achieve self-sufficiency in AI infrastructure and reduce dependency on foreign chip suppliers. It could reshape the global AI hardware market and accelerate China's digital economy, while also setting a precedent for national-scale computing resource pooling. The plan is part of Beijing's 'Six Networks' infrastructure initiative, aiming to integrate scattered regional computing resources into a unified network. Telecom operators like China Telecom and China Unicom have already launched token-based computing packages, selling computing power like mobile data to pave the way for large-scale AI applications.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 9, 10:09

**Background**: China's national computing power network is a key component of the 'East Data West Computing' project and the '15th Five-Year Plan' (2026-2030). It aims to build a unified computing resource scheduling system, similar to how the power grid distributes electricity, to support AI, big data, and cloud computing. The push for domestic chips reflects ongoing U.S. export restrictions on advanced semiconductors to China.

Alibaba has initiated discussions with Chinese state-owned nuclear enterprises to explore building small modular reactors (SMRs) to power its Renhe data center in Hangzhou. SMRs typically generate up to 300 MW per unit, about one-third of a conventional nuclear reactor, making them suitable for localized data center power needs.
