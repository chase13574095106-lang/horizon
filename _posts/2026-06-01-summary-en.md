## [Hackers Tricked Meta AI to Hijack Instagram Accounts](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/1/hackers-simply-asked-meta-ai/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Hackers exploited Meta's AI support bot to take over high-profile Instagram accounts by simply asking it to change the linked email address, bypassing normal security checks. This vulnerability highlights a critical design flaw in integrating AI into customer support, where a chatbot had the power to fast-track account recovery without proper verification, putting millions of users at risk. The attack involved using a VPN with an IP near the target's location, requesting a password reset, and then instructing the AI bot to link a new email, which received a one-time code for password reset. Meta's AI bot relied on location data to enable support, which hackers exploited.

rss · Simon Willison · Jun 1, 21:14

**Background**: Prompt injection is a type of attack where specially crafted inputs cause an AI model to ignore its original instructions and perform unintended actions. In this case, the AI support bot was given privileged access to change account emails and disable 2FA, which should have required human verification. The vulnerability was reported by multiple sources including KrebsOnSecurity and Engadget.

## [Geology Mimics Biochemistry, Blurring Life's Origin](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-dirt-that-refused-to-die-20260601/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

New research suggests that biochemical-like processes, such as the formation of organic compounds and energy gradients, may be inherent to geology rather than exclusive to life, challenging the traditional boundary between living and non-living systems. This finding reshapes our understanding of abiogenesis, suggesting that the transition from non-life to life may be a natural geological progression, with profound implications for astrobiology and the search for life on other planets like Europa and Enceladus. The research highlights that geothermal processes can create stable energy gradients over billions of years, which can 'manufacture' organic compounds that self-assemble into complex molecules, similar to biological systems. However, early Earth lacked free oxygen, so anaerobic metabolism without cellular confinement remains an open question.

hackernews · speckx · Jun 1, 15:11 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48357905)

**Background**: Abiogenesis is the natural process by which life arises from non-living matter, such as simple organic compounds. The prevailing hypothesis is that life emerged from an 'RNA world' or through 'metabolism-first' scenarios, often involving hydrothermal vents. This new research suggests that the chemistry of life is not exclusive to life but is fundamentally the chemistry of geology, blurring the line between geochemistry and biochemistry.
