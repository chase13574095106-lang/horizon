## [OpenAI releases 722 math manuscripts generated by internal frontier model](https://t.me/zaihuapd/44741) ⭐️ 9.0/10

OpenAI published a GitHub collection of 722 math manuscripts and 372 result series produced by an internal, unreleased frontier model, addressing long-standing open problems. Many of the proofs have been formally verified in Lean, and the repository notes that the model used about 3 hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute per result, attempted roughly 4,000 problems during evaluation, and provided 10 reasoning summaries. This is a major milestone for AI-for-math, showing that frontier models can generate formally verifiable proofs for open problems and potentially shifting how automated theorem proving and mathematical discovery are done. It could affect mathematicians, proof-assistant developers, and anyone relying on AI for rigorous scientific reasoning. The results are not all final: some remain under verification, and the model's natural-language write-ups have been described by mathematicians as unconventional, raising questions about readability and peer-review standards. The repository provides only 10 reasoning summaries for 372 result series, so transparency into the model's reasoning is limited.

**Background**: Lean is an interactive theorem prover that lets mathematicians write proofs in a formal language so every logical step can be checked by computer. Automated theorem proving is a long-standing AI subfield focused on having programs discover proofs; recent work combines large language models with proof assistants like Lean to generate candidate proofs that are then formally verified. OpenAI's release tests whether a frontier model can contribute to genuinely open mathematical problems rather than benchmark exercises.

## [Google Releases Gemini 4 Argon with 87% Physical Reasoning Score](https://t.me/zaihuapd/44743) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Google has released Gemini 4 Argon, a new-generation multimodal reasoning model that strengthens physical understanding, code generation, and visual-semantic reasoning. It achieves an 87% score on the ERQA embodied reasoning benchmark using pure reasoning, and can control robots such as Boston Dynamics' Spot to perform tasks like picking up and throwing objects. This release signals Google's push to lead in embodied AI, where models must reason about the physical world to control robots. The demonstrated improvement in real-world robot task success from 56% to 75% and reduced task time from 35 to 24 seconds shows tangible impact for robotics and automation. Gemini 4 Argon is available through the google-genai SDK, with pricing at $2 per million input tokens and $2 per million output tokens (though some sources list $4/$20). It can also generate simulation data to improve real-robot success rates, and has a 1M-token context window according to third-party benchmarks.

## [Python's experimental JIT compiler to be removed from CPython](https://lwn.net/SubscriberLink/1102102/0c00e4d550f25f8c/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

CPython's experimental JIT compiler, which was merged into the main development branch in early 2024 and refined in Python 3.15, is being removed following a new PEP request. The decision comes after significant progress in JIT optimizations such as automatic refcount operation removal and type-based specializations. The removal affects Python's performance roadmap, particularly the hope that JIT would offset the overhead of free-threading, and shifts focus to alternative optimization strategies like Cython, Numba, and PyPy. It also raises questions about CPython's approach to major performance features and community governance. The JIT was an experimental feature enabled via the --enable-experimental-jit configure flag, and its removal follows PEP 836, which proposes maintaining the JIT outside CPython or creating a pluggable JIT infrastructure. The decision was influenced by bureaucratic processes and the availability of other performance solutions.

hackernews · f311a · Oct 27, 11:17 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50257419)

**Background**: A JIT (just-in-time) compiler dynamically translates Python bytecode into machine code at runtime to improve execution speed, contrasting with the traditional interpreter. CPython's experimental JIT was introduced in Python 3.13 as part of efforts to boost performance, alongside free-threading (removing the GIL). PEP 744 originally outlined the JIT plan, but subsequent discussions led to PEP 836, which re-evaluates its future.

## [Chinese team claims 3.2 Pb/s real-time optical fiber transmission record](https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/3-2-pb-s-real-time-optical-fiber-transfer-speed-world-record-claimed-by-chinese-team-speed-fast-enough-to-download-more-than-20-000-4k-movies-in-one-second) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A Chinese research team has demonstrated a real-time optical fiber transmission rate of 3.2 Pb/s using a 24-core fiber, enough to transfer over 20,000 4K movies in one second. This surpasses the team's previous record of 2.5 Pb/s set earlier this year by nearly 30%. This milestone demonstrates practical advances in space-division multiplexing and multi-band transmission using commercially compatible hardware, which could accelerate the development of higher-capacity optical networks. It shows that record-breaking speeds are moving closer to real-world deployability, impacting telecommunications infrastructure and future network design. The test employed a commercially compatible hardware architecture and validated space-division multiplexing (SDM) and S+C+L multi-band transmission techniques. While laboratory records have reached 5.29 Pb/s, those used advanced or experimental equipment and signal processing, whereas this result emphasizes practical compatibility.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 27, 03:45

**Background**: Space-division multiplexing (SDM) is a technique that uses multiple spatial channels, such as multiple cores in a single fiber, to transmit independent data streams and increase total capacity. S+C+L band transmission refers to using the S-band (short wavelength), C-band (conventional), and L-band (long wavelength) together to expand the usable optical spectrum. Multi-core fibers and multi-band transmission are key approaches to overcoming the capacity limits of standard single-mode fibers.
