## [OpenAI Announces AI-Assisted Breakthroughs in Math, CS, and Physics](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/) ⭐️ 10.0/10

OpenAI announced three groundbreaking discoveries: a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), and AI-generated natural language proofs for Yang-Mills existence and Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD). The Yang-Mills proof was produced on September 9, 2026, followed by the QCD proof on September 18, 2026, with Lean formalization completed on October 16, 2026. If validated, these results would overturn a foundational assumption in computational complexity theory and represent the first AI-generated proofs of major open problems in mathematical physics, potentially reshaping how research is conducted in these fields. The involvement of AI in solving Millennium Prize-level problems signals a significant shift in the role of machine learning in scientific discovery. The ETH disproof presents a deterministic algorithm for exactly counting satisfying assignments of k-CNF formulas that runs in time (n+m)^{O_k(1)} 2^{n/(log n)^{1/2+o(1)}}, which is superpolynomial but subexponential, contradicting ETH. The Yang-Mills and QCD proofs were formalized in Lean, a proof assistant, adding rigor to the AI-generated arguments.

## [Preprint Claims to Disprove the Exponential Time Hypothesis](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf) ⭐️ 10.0/10

A preprint hosted on OpenAI's GitHub claims to disprove the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), a foundational conjecture in computational complexity theory. The paper, dated October 25, 2026, has sparked intense discussion online about its potential implications. If correct, this would be a paradigm-shifting result in complexity theory, as ETH underpins many conditional lower bounds for algorithms. It could lead to faster algorithms for SAT and related problems, though the practical impact remains uncertain. The paper notes that runtime bounds of the form 2^o(n) do not transfer under general polynomial-time reductions, so the result does not imply that general NP problems admit subexponential time algorithms. The claim is a disproof of ETH, not a proof that P=NP.

## [Northwestern traps a single positron for months in a quantum cyclotron](https://www.wired.com/story/scientists-trapped-an-antimatter-particle-for-months-to-study-its-secrets/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Gerald Gabrielse's team at Northwestern University used a one-positron quantum cyclotron to suspend a single positron—the antimatter counterpart of the electron—in a Penning trap at near-absolute-zero temperatures for months without destroying it, allowing repeated measurements. The work, published in Physical Review Letters, demonstrates quantum control of the positron's energy levels and is expected to improve antimatter measurement precision by about 30 times. This enables a direct, high-precision comparison of the positron's magnetic moment with that of the electron, providing one of the most stringent tests of charge-parity-time (CPT) symmetry and the standard model's prediction that matter and antimatter behave identically. Any measured difference would force a rewrite of the laws governing matter and antimatter and could help explain why the universe is dominated by matter. The positron is cooled and held in a Penning trap using electromagnetic fields, and carefully tuned microwaves are used to add precise amounts of energy and detect individual quantum transitions, making the particle behave like an artificial atom. So far no unexpected difference between positron and electron has been observed, and the technique complements the LSym experiment, which aims at a simultaneous particle–antiparticle comparison.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 26, 06:06

**Background**: A positron is the antimatter counterpart of an electron, with the same mass but opposite charge, and it annihilates on contact with ordinary matter, making it extremely difficult to store. A Penning trap uses static electric and magnetic fields to confine charged particles in a vacuum, and a quantum cyclotron is a single trapped particle whose energy can only change in discrete steps that can be triggered and observed one by one. Comparing the magnetic moments of the electron and positron is a classic test of whether matter and antimatter obey the same physical laws.
