## [Science Probe Alleges Unreported Gene-Editing Death at Shanghai Xinhua Hospital](https://t.me/zaihuapd/45103) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Science magazine published an exclusive investigation on July 23, 2026, alleging that a 6-year-old girl died in late March 2025 after receiving an experimental base-editing gene therapy at Shanghai Jiao Tong University-affiliated Xinhua Hospital, and that the event was never publicly disclosed. The child, who had a rare single-base mutation disorder, received trillions of AAV viral vectors injected into her spinal fluid to target brain neurons and died seven days later from a severe immune reaction. The report raises serious concerns about regulatory oversight and research ethics in China's fast-growing gene-therapy sector, potentially eroding public trust and prompting calls for stricter clinical-trial transparency. It also highlights the risks of AAV-based delivery and the importance of mandatory trial registration and adverse-event reporting. The parents reportedly paid more than $800,000 out of pocket, and the ClinicalTrials.gov record for the trial had not been updated for over a year. The case involved direct central nervous system delivery of AAV vectors at high doses, a route known to carry elevated immune-risk profiles.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 13, 09:24

**Background**: Base editing is a newer form of CRISPR-based genome editing that chemically changes single DNA letters without making double-strand breaks, and it is still largely experimental. AAV (adeno-associated virus) vectors are commonly used to deliver gene therapies but can trigger immune responses, especially at high doses or when injected directly into the central nervous system. ClinicalTrials.gov is a U.S. registry that many journals and regulators require for trial transparency, though compliance varies globally.

## [FAA Issues Nationwide Ground Stop Over Telecom Outage](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/faa-orders-nationwide-ground-stop-departing-flights-reasons-safety-rcna609247) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On November 13, 2026, the Federal Aviation Administration issued a nationwide ground stop halting all departing flights in the United States due to 'extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety.' The outage was later reported to affect a telecommunications network managed by L3Harris Technologies that handles critical radio frequencies used by air-traffic controllers, and the ground stop was subsequently lifted. A nationwide ground stop is an extremely rare event that disrupts thousands of flights and strands passengers across the country, highlighting the fragility of critical infrastructure that modern aviation depends on. It also raises concerns about the resilience of communication systems and the potential risks of integrating new technologies like AI into air-traffic control. The FAA advisory stated that traffic should remain ground stopped until further notice, and the outage appeared to affect radio frequencies used by controllers to communicate with pilots, though it was unclear whether radar feeds were also impacted. The ground stop lasted for several hours, with departures at major airports like LAX and JFK halted, and officials indicated the issue might not be resolved quickly.

A ground stop is a traffic management initiative that halts departures at airports, typically due to severe weather, security threats, or system outages. L3Harris Technologies is a major defense and aerospace contractor that provides communication systems for air-traffic control. The FAA has recently been testing AI-based tools, such as the SMART system, to assist controllers and reduce delays.

## [AI Deciphers Gödel's Shorthand Notebooks, Revealing Hundreds of Theorems](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths) ⭐️ 8.0/10

AI has been used to decipher Kurt Gödel's shorthand notebooks, reportedly uncovering hundreds of proved theorems and 14 new themes in logic. The notebooks were written in Gabelsberger shorthand, an obsolete German stenography system. This discovery could shed new light on the work of one of the 20th century's greatest logicians and potentially influence modern logic and mathematics. It also demonstrates AI's growing role in historical and archival research. The shorthand, Gabelsberger, was already obsolete during Gödel's lifetime, making decipherment challenging. The findings include hundreds of theorems and new logical themes, but the consistency and completeness of the notebooks have not been commented on by AI.

**Background**: Kurt Gödel (1906–1978) was an Austrian-American logician famous for his incompleteness theorems, which revolutionized mathematical logic. He left behind extensive notebooks written in Gabelsberger shorthand, a system that few could read. AI techniques are increasingly used to transcribe and analyze historical manuscripts.

## [OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet for AI agent payments](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet in partnership with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, allowing AI agents to make tokenized payments without exposing users' real card numbers. Starting today, it is available to ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over in the US via dots, requiring a US-issued card. This is a major step toward enabling AI agents to autonomously complete purchases, potentially transforming how users interact with e-commerce and digital services. The involvement of all three major payment networks signals strong industry backing and could accelerate the adoption of agentic payments across the ecosystem. Each purchase requires per-transaction verification: users review the item and amount, then confirm with Face ID or fingerprint, while the payment network issues a token that replaces the real card number. The initial rollout is limited to US Pro users on dots, and no community discussion was provided.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 13, 15:43

**Background**: Payment tokenization is a security process that replaces sensitive card data with a unique identifier, reducing the risk of data theft. AI agents are increasingly being developed to perform tasks autonomously, but payment has been a missing piece; OpenAI's dots is a product that helps agents handle work tasks. This launch builds on the growing trend of agentic payments, such as Google's Agent Payments Protocol (AP2).
