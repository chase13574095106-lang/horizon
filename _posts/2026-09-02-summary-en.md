## [LZ Detector Observes Single Anomalous Event, Hints at New Physics](https://www.science.org/content/article/world-s-biggest-dark-matter-detector-spots-single-weird-particle) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The LUX-ZEPLIN (LZ) dark matter detector, the world's largest, has observed a single unusual particle event that could potentially indicate new physics beyond the Standard Model. The collaboration published the result, but emphasizes that more data is needed to confirm any discovery. This event, if confirmed, could challenge current understanding of dark matter and particle physics, potentially pointing to new particles or interactions. It underscores the sensitivity of next-generation detectors and the ongoing search for dark matter, which constitutes about 27% of the universe. The LZ detector is located 1,480 meters underground in the Sanford Underground Research Facility in a former gold mine in South Dakota. The event is not yet statistically significant (likely below 5 sigma), and the collaboration is collecting more data to determine its nature.

hackernews · randycupertino · Sep 2, 13:40 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49536079)

**Background**: Dark matter is an invisible form of matter that does not emit or absorb light, inferred from gravitational effects. Weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs) are a leading candidate for dark matter, and detectors like LZ use liquid xenon to look for rare WIMP-nucleus interactions. The LZ experiment combines data from 2022 and 2024 runs, setting world-leading limits on WIMP interactions.

## [Paint.NET Rewrites Direct2D for Wine with Claude's Help](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/2/rick-brewster/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Rick Brewster announced that Paint.NET now includes an internal, from-scratch, clean-room reverse-engineered rewrite of Direct2D, used when running on Wine via the /wine flag. This rewrite was largely generated by Anthropic's Claude AI and is contained in PaintDotNet.Windows.Direct2D1.Managed.dll. This achievement demonstrates the potential of AI-assisted development for complex, low-level software engineering, potentially enabling other applications to run on Wine or Linux. It also highlights the growing role of 'vibe coding' in real-world projects, even for critical components. The rewrite consists of approximately 180,000 lines of code, which Brewster admits he could not thoroughly review, describing it as 'vibe coded' and 'trust me bro' style. He had to actively supervise Claude to ensure correct resource management, such as proper AddRef() calls for COM objects, and to correct architectural decisions.

rss · Simon Willison · Sep 2, 05:50

**Background**: Direct2D is a native Windows API for 2D graphics, and Wine's implementation has been incomplete, hindering applications like Paint.NET. A clean-room reverse-engineered rewrite means the code was written from scratch based on observed behavior, not copied from Microsoft's source. 'Vibe coding' refers to software development where an AI generates code based on prompts, with the developer often not fully reviewing every line.

This incident underscores the growing problem of ID document leaks and the challenges of protecting such data.
